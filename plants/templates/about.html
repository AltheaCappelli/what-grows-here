I recently moved to Düsseldorf, and for the first time I had a balcony in my apartment. As a plant enthtusiast, I was excited to start a little balcony garden! But I quickly realized I had no idea which plants were suitable for my balcony, and unsurprisingly half of them died in no time :D This gave me the idea for this web app, as I could not find such an interactive map online focused on balcony gardening in Europe.
Using Django, a Python web framework, I created this application to help myself and other wannabe balcony gardeners.
P.S. As I am not a botanist, I collected plants information from various sources online. If you have any suggestions or corrections, please feel free to reach out to me!
